Objects with personality

Twelve everyday things friends ride in, bring along, or gather around. One color, rounded, bold enough to hold up as a 20px favicon.

Pick
01Van

The ride everyone piles into. Headlights double as eyes. Friends going somewhere, together.

Pick
02Walkie-talkie

Come in, over. The friend on the other end, no feed in between. Short range on purpose.

Pick
03Boombox

Bring the music. Two speakers read as eyes and the tape slot smiles back.

04Die

A five that rearranged itself into a smile. Game night, in one shape.

05Backpack

Packed and ready by the door. The front pocket carries the grin.

06Sneaker

Just show up Saturday at 10. Laced, already moving.

07Tent

Someone got there first. Two eyes peek out of the door.

08Folded map

The folds make an M. A dotted route that ends at X, where the plan happens.

09Cheers

Two mugs meeting in the middle. Coffee, tea, or a pint, it counts.

10Marshmallow

Happy on a stick. Pairs with the s'mores scene already in the campaign.

11Lantern

The light that keeps the night going. A small flame, no screen glow.

12Popsicle

One bite in, one drip on the way. A long summer afternoon.

As a family

Any one could lead, with the rest living on as stickers, merch, and interest moments in the palette.
