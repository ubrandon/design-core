Three ways to lock it

How strict should it get?

All three use the same Flower of Life grid, the same hexagram and only φ sizes. They differ in how the right foot lands and which way the spiral turns.

Keeps today’s forward step. The right foot is the one circle held by the baseline, not a grid point.

1.42 : 1
