Six relations

  1. 1

    Kepler frame

    The mark fills a rectangle of √φ to 1. Its diagonals and their perpendiculars cross exactly on the golden cuts, the four eyes.

  2. 2

    One ladder

    Every radius is the hump radius divided by √φ a whole number of times. Two steps down is exactly one φ.

  3. 3

    Left counter on an eye

    The left counter is centered on the lower left eye, 0.382 across and 0.618 down.

  4. 4

    Notch touches both humps

    The notch uses step 5, the same as the left counter, and touches both humps, so the hump spacing comes from the ladder.

  5. 5

    The drop hangs on a line

    The drop is centered straight under the right edge of the notch and touches the right counter.

  6. 6

    Right counter meets its foot

    The right counter touches the right foot and shares its center line. The side arcs are R times φ⁴ and touch hump and foot.
