The thinking behind G / Proposed brand story

two people.
one plan.

MixedRoutes gets friends from the group chat to the same table. The mark is that moment: two circles pulled close enough to become one shape.

01 / The big circle

Someone has an idea.

The larger circle is the friend who says let's do something. Grounded, low, and ready to pull people in.

02 / The small circle

Someone says yes.

The smaller circle sits higher and to the right, already on the way. It is the reply that turns a maybe into a plan.

03 / The neck

That's the plan.

Where the two shapes pull together, they stop being two dots. One connected silhouette that holds up from a billboard down to a 16px icon.

Meeting is the meaning.

Two dots on a map are just two people. Pull them together and it becomes a plan. The mark does not spell the name, it shows the moment MixedRoutes exists for.

Soft, with direction.

Two unequal circles on a rising diagonal. The size difference and the angle keep it from reading as a peanut or a dumbbell, and give it a sense of going somewhere.

One color. Any surface.

Meet is a single solid shape, so it takes any color from the V2 palette. Blue leads by default. Lilac, mint, apricot, and ink let interests and photography take over without changing the mark.

Two circles. Always fused.

Keep both circles and the neck intact. Never pull them apart, level, or mirror the shape. Use V2 blue on quiet light surfaces, and a single contrasting color when photography or interest colors lead.
