02 / The thinking behind B

one shape.
room for everyone.

Most plans fall apart in the group chat. MixedRoutes is where they hold together. The mark is a single soft M with nothing to pull apart: one place, shaped to take people in.

01 / The shoulders

Two sides, leaning in.

The M rises in two rounded peaks that tilt toward each other, like two friends turning to talk. Neither side is bigger than the other.

02 / The middle

A seat saved for you.

Where the peaks meet, the shape dips and then holds. It is the gap at the table that someone pulls a chair into. The M is built around the space between people.

03 / The feet

Planted and steady.

Wide, rounded feet sit firmly on the ground. The shape feels weighty and reliable: a regular spot you can count on, not a one-off.

The belonging is the meaning.

There is no seam, no gap and no sharp corner anywhere in the mark. It is one continuous form, soft enough to feel friendly and solid enough to feel like home. Find your people, then keep coming back.

Soft on purpose.

Rounded everywhere, the M avoids the hard edges of utility apps. It looks like something you would put on a mug, not a dashboard.

Unmistakable in silhouette.

One solid shape survives any size, any color and any material. It is the easiest of the three to stamp, stitch or cut.

03 / At every size

from billboard to favicon.

A logo earns its keep at the small end. These are the sizes it lives at in the app and the browser.

04 / In use

where people will meet it.

Three everyday touchpoints with identical content across concepts. Only the mark changes.

05 / Out in the world

at your usual spot.
