The thinking behind C / Proposed brand story

different pieces.
more life together.

MixedRoutes brings what you love, who you know, and what you could do into the same place. The mark gives that mix a simple visual form: three distinct pieces, belonging together.

01 / The circle

It starts with you.

The circle can stand for a person, a place on the map, or the spark of an interest. It is the smallest piece, but it belongs just as much: a coffee with one friend counts as much as a big night out.

02 / The diagonal

A route into something.

The angled piece brings a sense of movement. It suggests reaching beyond the familiar: following an interest, finding common ground, or taking an idea one step closer to a real experience.

03 / The upright

Something to come together for.

The upright gives the mark a steady anchor. It can represent the place, person, or plan that makes a possibility feel real. A grounded counterweight to the movement of the diagonal.

The mix is the meaning.

Together, the pieces suggest a loose, rounded M without becoming identical or merging into one solid block. That is the idea of MixedRoutes: different people and interests finding a shared experience, while keeping what makes each one distinct.

Open space, soft edges.

The gaps let each piece remain legible. Rounded ends give the whole mark an approachable feel, while the asymmetry keeps it lively. It feels like an invitation to join in, with room for different kinds of people.

Many interests. One identity.

Apricot, lilac, and blue connect the full-color mark to the wider V2 palette. They express variety, rather than assigning each piece a permanent interest category. The same story holds in one color because the relationship between the shapes does the work.

Three shapes. Always one recognizable mark.

Keep the silhouette and spacing consistent. Use the full-color signature on quiet light surfaces, and a single contrasting color when photography or interest colors lead.
