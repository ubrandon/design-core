The thinking behind B2 / Proposed brand story

one shape.
always in motion.

MixedRoutes gets you from thinking about it to actually going. The mark gives that a simple visual form: one soft M, caught mid-step.

01 / The raised hump

Someone goes first.

One side lifts higher, like a foot already off the ground. It is the friend who suggests the plan, the small nudge that turns a maybe into a let's go.

02 / The arch

A way in.

The open arch underneath works like a doorway. There is always room to step inside, whether it is a coffee for two or a night out with twenty.

03 / The whole shape

Everyone, in one piece.

B2 is a single connected silhouette, not a set of parts. Different people, one plan, moving together. It holds up from a billboard down to a 16px icon.

The bounce is the meaning.

A plain bubble M feels friendly but generic. The lift on one side adds just enough motion to say what MixedRoutes is for: getting out of the group chat and into the plan, together.

Soft, but never static.

The rounded shape keeps all the warmth of the original B. The offset humps and the narrow notch between them give it rhythm, so it never reads as a plain bubble letter.

One color. Any surface.

B2 is a single solid shape, so it takes any color from the V2 palette. Blue leads by default. Lilac, mint, apricot, and ink let interests and photography take over without changing the mark.

One shape. Always one recognizable mark.

Keep the silhouette and the lifted hump intact. Never mirror or level it. Use V2 blue on quiet light surfaces, and a single contrasting color when photography or interest colors lead.
