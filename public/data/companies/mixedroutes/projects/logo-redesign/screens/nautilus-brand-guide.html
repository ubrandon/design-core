How it's built

Nautilus is drawn from circles and one golden spiral, so every curve has a reason and every size is a ratio of every other.

  1. 1

    One ladder of sizes

    Each circle is the hump radius divided by √φ a whole number of times. The humps differ by exactly φ: the small left hump is step 2, the big right hump is step 0.

  2. 2

    Riding the golden spiral

    Where the outline meets the spiral, the circle has the spiral's curve, so the whole left side and the right shoulder run along it.

  3. 3

    Leaning forward

    The small left hump and the big right one give the M its lean. In motion it shifts that weight to the other side and back.

Two states

The master M never changes. The moving M sways and turns into your interests, which is how the brand comes alive. Knowing which one to use is the whole system.

Master M

Fixed, everywhere people need to recognize us

App icon, favicon, app store listing, legal pages, and any first meeting with the brand. Blue on white or white on ink. It never morphs.

Moving M

Alive, where people already know us

App launch, loading, plan moments, the website hero, social, and campaigns. One object at a time, and it always comes home to the M.

Ways to work it in

Moments where the moving M earns its place. Each one ties the object to something real: an interest, a plan, a city, a person.
