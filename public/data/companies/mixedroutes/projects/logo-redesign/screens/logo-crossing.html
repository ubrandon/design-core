miedroutes

Two routes cross. That is the name, the idea and the logo in one shape.

The symbol

The x in mixedroutes.

Two people on different routes, and the one place they meet. The symbol is lifted straight out of the name, so the logo and the wordmark are one system.

9/10

The strongest idea so far.

Nothing in the category looks like it, it explains the name, and it works as an icon, a wordmark letter, a sticker and a pattern.

  • Two strokes, two colors, one crossing. Simple enough to draw from memory.
  • The warm route always runs on top, so the mark has depth without gradients.
  • Holds at 20 px: two curves stay two curves.
  • An X can read as close or delete. The curves and the color keep it friendly, but test it next to a real close button.
  • X is also a social network. Ours is soft and two-tone, theirs is sharp and mono, but keep an eye on it.

Color

Two routes, many colors.

Blue stays the anchor. The second route borrows from the V2 palette, so every colorway still feels like MixedRoutes.
