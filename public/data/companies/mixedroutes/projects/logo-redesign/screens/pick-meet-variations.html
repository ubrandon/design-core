Meet, 8 ways

Two circles pulled together. Variations push it toward an M, toward more people, and toward more character.

01Meet

The original. A big circle and a small one fused at the neck.

02Duo

Split into two colors, so it reads as two people, not one blob.

03Joining

A third dot on its way in. Plans are forming.

04Meet M

Three circles fuse into an M. The low middle one makes the V.

Meet M with taller legs and a lower middle circle. Reads as M first.

06Trio

Three circles in a row, big to small. A group on the move.

07Buddies

Add eyes and the two circles become friends leaning in.

08Outline

Same shape drawn as a line. Lighter, more like a route.

Does Meet look like other logos?

Mostly clear. One app is close on style and color, and one owns the outline version.

Checked about 170 App Store icons from social, plans, maps, meetup, and dating searches, plus Mastercard, Peanut, Two Dots, Bump, Zenly, Howbout, Partiful, Luma, Timeleft, and Meetup. No app or brand uses Meet's exact shape: a big and a small circle fused on a rising diagonal. This is a design check, not a trademark search.
