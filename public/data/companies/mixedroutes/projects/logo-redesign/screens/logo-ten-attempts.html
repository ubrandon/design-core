Why none of these is a 10 yet.

A 10 is earned, not drawn. It needs to win a five-second recall test against the category and hold up after a year on real screens. Cheer is the closest, because it keeps the letter people already like and gives it a meaning. Next step: put Cheer and Folded Map next to 10 rival app icons and see which one people remember.
