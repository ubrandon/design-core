Two states

The master M never changes. The moving M is how the brand comes alive. Knowing which one to use is the whole system.

Master M

Fixed, everywhere people need to recognize us

App icon, favicon, app store listing, legal pages, and any first meeting with the brand. Blue on white or white on ink. It never morphs.

Moving M

Alive, where people already know us

App launch, loading, plan moments, the website hero, social, and campaigns. One object at a time, and it always comes home to the M.

Rules

Constant M, changing objects. The system only works while the M stays easy to recognize.

Do

  • Always come back to the M.
  • Show one object at a time.
  • Keep objects solid, with details as cutouts.
  • Match the object to real content: the plan, the interest, the city, the person.
  • Use the interest color for each object, white or blue for the M.

Don't

  • Morph the app icon, favicon, or first touchpoints.
  • Add faces or turn objects into characters.
  • Use gradients, outlines, shadows, or 3D.
  • Loop behind dense text or next to important data.
  • Use objects as decoration with no link to what is on screen.
