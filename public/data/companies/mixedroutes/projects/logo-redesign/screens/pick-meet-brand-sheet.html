Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark equal to the small circle's diameter. Nothing else goes in the shaded zone.

Minimum size

The mark holds its shape down to 14 px. Below 24 px, use the mark without the wordmark.

Color and type

Lilac becomes the signature. Ink carries the mark. Neon blue stays for actions so the product does not need to change.
