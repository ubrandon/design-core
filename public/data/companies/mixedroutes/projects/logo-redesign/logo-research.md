# Logo research: simple, bold, memorable

## Findings

- Discord simplified Clyde's small protrusions because they did not reproduce well on small surfaces. Symmetry and a standalone silhouette improved flexibility, while expressive features retained personality. Source: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009438682-A-Fresh-New-Look-to-Celebrate-Our-6th-Birthday
- Dropbox replaced its illustrative logo with a reduced glyph. Its designers emphasize sturdy shapes, recognition at large and small sizes, and color or monochrome reproduction. Source: https://blog.dropbox.com/topics/company/simplify-your-brand
- Pentagram describes Slack's identity as designed for consistent performance across scales and contexts. Source: https://www.pentagram.com/work/slack
- Paul Rand describes identification as the logo's purpose and connects its reputation to the company behind it. Source: https://www.paulrand.design/life/interviews/1988-id-magazine.html

## Application to MixedRoutes

Our synthesis, not a universal scoring formula: a great mark combines simplicity with a distinctive detail. It should identify the brand, suggest its personality, and remain useful across applications. It does not need to depict every app feature. Recognition also grows through consistent use and the experience associated with the company.

Review criteria: identifiable silhouette; easy visual recall; clear at small sizes; works in one color; friendly and adventurous personality; balanced spacing and curves. Practical checks for refinement: view at 16, 24, and 32 pixels; compare solid black and reversed white versions; ask someone to sketch it after a brief look; compare against adjacent brands. These are proposed checks, not claimed user-test results.

The previous people-inside-pins, hands, and tables overexplained the product. This round reduces the idea to a compact symbol. Scout offers a curious companion; Gather suggests belonging in a compact three-lobed form. Link and Hello are readable but more generic. Wander is recognizable but its thin tips need attention at tiny sizes. Spark feels energetic but occupies a crowded visual category.

Six concepts are raster ideation studies made with built-in image_gen. Small generated variants are not perfectly geometry-matched, especially Spark. A selected concept needs consistent vector construction and actual small-size checks before production. Distinctiveness is an initial design judgment, not a trademark clearance claim.
