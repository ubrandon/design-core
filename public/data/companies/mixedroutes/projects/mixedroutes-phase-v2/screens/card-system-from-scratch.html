Card system · from scratch

Three primitives, not thirteen cards

Tile for anything you can discover, Plan card for anything people committed to, Row for anything the app asks or tells you. Every old variant maps onto one primitive plus an accent: a facepile means friends, a spark means AI, a count means action needed. Chrome is reserved for commitment, so plans are the only elevated surface on any screen.

Rules

What keeps three primitives from drifting back into thirteen cards.

Chrome = commitmentOnly plan cards get elevation and the dark ambient surface. Tiles and rows stay flat on the page.
Facepile = friendsFriend proof is always the same 20px facepile in the meta line, never a separate card type.
Spark = AI, with a whyAI content uses the gradient spark plus a plain-language reason chip. No sparkle without a reason.
Save lives top rightOne position, one style, two states. Saved flips to white with a blue glyph.
One action per cardJoin on plans, one button per row, tiles are fully tappable. Choices move to the detail view.
One text scaleKicker 11 caps, title 14 to 18 by size, meta 12.5. Radii: tiles 18, rows 20, plans 20 to 24.
Accents, not themesGreen for counts, gradient for AI, amber for stars. Everything else stays ink on white.

In context: the four layouts

Every screen composes the same primitives four ways: tile carousel, plan carousel, tile grid, and row list. Sizes are fixed per layout, so a tile is 172 in a rail, 240 wide when featured, half-width in a grid, and never anything else.
