# Landing page E: plans that actually happen

September 14, 2026. Mounted right of Landing page D. Built from docs/game-feel-for-digital-products.md (read in full), the V2 brand, and the feature set. Hero, then nine sections with one idea and one artifact each, then a settled ending. Page classes use the wpe- prefix in company.css; card silhouettes reuse hst- and ml- so they match the app.

## Review contract (section 30)

- Purpose: help the person decide to get the app.
- Primary read: "plans that actually happen." and a fan of real things to do with a friend already going.
- Necessary support: one blue button per viewport, platform line, one proof line.
- Optional depth: FAQ and About in the nav and footer.
- State changes: none on a static page. The sections show the product loop's states instead.

## The sections follow the loop in section 12 and the rhythm in section 20

Understand the situation, choose, act, see the result, rest.

1. Ask: express an intention in plain words (section 17, "confidence that ordinary language works").
2. Find: the result card says why it is here; the whole card is the target (sections 5 and 9).
3. Friends: see who is going, join with one tap.
4. Saved: what friends love, ready to plan.
5. Plan: a time and a place is the whole commitment (section 11, action as an understandable commitment).
6. Share: one link, no app needed to view.
7. RSVP: Going, Maybe, Can't are options that differ (section 11 and 14, a real choice).
8. Confirmed: the changed plan card is the evidence, with Change attached to it (section 12, "make the changed object the primary evidence", undo on the change).
9. Chat: the conversation stays with its object (section 8, keep the label, value, and action together).
10. Rest: "see you there." and one button. One ending only (sections 18 and 20, peak-end and closure).

## Other rules applied

- Copy (section 6): headings under six words, one line of support, no restating what the card shows, one verb per operation.
- Emphasis (section 7): the nav CTA is a text link so the hero button is the only strong signal in the first viewport; one blue action per section.
- State vocabulary (section 12): blue is action, citrus is a date, mint is confirmed, apricot is saved by friends, interest tags use interest colours.

Names, saves, counts, and the share URL are illustrative, as in the other V2 screens.
