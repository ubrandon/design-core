Principles

Useful, then beautiful.

Every flourish behaves like information: a blaze marks the route, a stamp marks the edition, a rule separates the facts.

Texture has a source.

Paper grain, ink, map lines, tape and pencil are welcome. Generic digital noise and glossy glass effects are not.

One trail marker.

Blaze orange appears once per composition. Charcoal does the daily work; orange tells you where to go next.

Earned imperfection.

Layouts may feel pinned, cropped, or annotated, while type and interaction remain crisp and easy to scan.

Signature object · the field card

Good plans leave a mark.

The card feels like a keepsake slipped into a notebook: portrait photography, a paper caption, blaze rule, serif name, and compact field data. It works before the plan as an invitation and after it as a memory.

This direction makes MixedRoutes feel grounded, considered, and outdoors-capable without becoming an adventure brand. The test: would the surface still feel useful with no screen glow at all?
