Why we’re here

The best nights
of your life won’t
happen on
your phone.

Every app you open is designed to keep you there. Feeds, followers, streaks, and an algorithm that always has one more thing for you. Meanwhile, “we should hang out” dies in a group chat for the fourth week in a row.

MixedRoutes exists for exactly one thing: turning a vague idea into a time, a place, and people who show up. Find something worth doing, get your people to a yes, lock in the details, and put your phone away.

The app does its job when you stop looking at it.

Brandon Founder, product
Eric Founder, engineer
