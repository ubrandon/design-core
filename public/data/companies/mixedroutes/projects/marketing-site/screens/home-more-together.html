All questions

Is MixedRoutes free?

Yes. MixedRoutes is free to use on iOS, Android, and the web. You can keep using it for free.

Can I choose who sees my plans?

Yes. Make a plan private, visible to friends, or public. You can turn link sharing on or off for each plan.

Can I use it on my computer?

Absolutely. Make plans and keep up with your groups in your browser. Everything stays in sync with the app.
