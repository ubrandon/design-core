Principles

Photos carry everything.

Every surface leads with a real moment in real light. The UI is a frame: warm neutrals, hairlines, and one amber accent.

Modern by restraint.

No aging tricks and no decoration. What keeps it current is confidence: big type, generous space, clean 14px radii.

Night is a section, not a mode.

Espresso bands appear where the content is an evening. The product itself stays light, warm, and readable.

Say it flat.

Headlines are short declarative sentences with a period. The heavy, tight setting makes plain words feel like conviction.
