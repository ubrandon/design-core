Is MixedRoutes free?−

Yes. MixedRoutes is free to use on iOS, Android, and the web. You can keep using it for free.

Do my friends need the app to join a plan?−

No app needed. Send a link and they can see the whole plan right in their browser. To RSVP they create a free account, on the web or in the app, and they're in. If they download the app later, everything is already there.

What can I use it on?−

iOS, Android, and the web. Everything stays in sync, so you can set up a plan from your laptop and run the group chat from your phone. Notifications work best in the app.

Who can see my plans?−

You decide, plan by plan. A plan can be private, visible to your friends, or public. You can also turn link sharing on or off for each plan whenever you want. Nothing is public unless you make it public.

Does MixedRoutes recommend events?−

Yes, based on interests you pick. MixedRoutes helps you keep up with friends' plans and find local events. The goal is to help you find something worth doing and make a plan.

How do I find things to do nearby?−

Pick a few interests and MixedRoutes surfaces real local events that match: concerts, markets, trivia nights, whatever your city has going on. See something good? Turn it into a plan and invite your people.

What are groups?−

Every friend group gets its own space, with its own chat and its own plans. The dinner plan, the trip talk, and the photos live where the group lives, so nothing important gets buried under 80 memes.

Will it blow up my phone with notifications?−

No. You hear from us when something actually needs you: a friend invites you, a plan changes, or your group is talking. Notifications exist to get people together, not to pull you back into the app.

What do you do with my data?−

We use it to run the app and nothing else. We don't sell it or share it with advertisers. If you import contacts to find friends, your address book is scrambled on your phone before anything leaves it, so we never see the real thing.

Can I delete my account?−

Yes, anytime, right in the app. You can download a copy of your data first, and deleting your account removes your information from MixedRoutes.
