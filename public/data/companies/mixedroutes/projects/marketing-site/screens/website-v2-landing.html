Your people

see what your
friends are up to.

Home is about the people you'd actually go with. Who's going where, what they saved, what you both have on your list, and a nudge when a group has gone quiet.

  • Say when you're down to hang. A status your friends can see, and nothing more than that.
  • Spot common ground. The place you both saved is one tap from a plan.
  • Answer invites right there. I'm in or Maybe, without opening anything.
  • Groups that stay alive. When it's been a while, the group gets a few ideas.

Plans

a time. a place.
people who show up.

Start from an event, a place, or your own idea. Give it a time and a place, invite your people, and keep the RSVPs, the chat, and the details in one spot.

  • Invites and RSVPs that are easy to find later.
  • A chat per plan, so the details never get buried.
  • Change notifications. New time or new place, everyone hears about it.
  • Share a link. Friends without the app can open the plan in a browser and RSVP with a free account.
  • You choose who sees it. Just the invited, your group, or public so others nearby can join.

Read the full FAQ

We already have a group chat.

Keep it. MixedRoutes is where the plan lives: the time, the place, and who's in, easy to find without scrolling back. The chat is for the chat.

My friends won't download another app.

They don't have to, to see the plan. Send the link and it opens in any browser. RSVPing takes a free account, which takes about a minute.

Is this another feed I'll get stuck in?

There's a Discover feed, and it's built around your interests and your friends, not around keeping you here. Every suggestion tells you why it's there, and you can reset them.

I don't go out much.

Good. Coffee with one friend, a walk, a game night at home all count. Quiet plans are most of the plans.

What does it cost?

MixedRoutes is free to use on iOS, Android, and web. You can keep using it for free.
