About

We win when you go.

MixedRoutes is a small team building the opposite of a social network.

The best nights of your life won't happen on your phone. So we built an app that gets you off it.

Every app you open is designed to keep you there. Feeds, followers, streaks, and an algorithm that always has one more thing for you. Meanwhile, “we should hang out” dies in a group chat for the fourth week in a row.

MixedRoutes exists for exactly one thing: turning a vague idea into a time, a place, and people who show up. Find something worth doing, get your people to a yes, lock in the details, and put your phone away. That's the whole product. The app did its job when you stop looking at it.
