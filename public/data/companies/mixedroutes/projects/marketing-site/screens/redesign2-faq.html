FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version: it's free, your friends don't need the app, and there is no algorithm.

Is MixedRoutes free?

Yes, completely. No ads, no premium tier, no data selling. We don't make money from your attention, so we don't need to trap it.

Do my friends need the app to join a plan?

No. Send a link and they see the plan and RSVP right in the browser, no app and no account needed. If they get the app later, they're already in.

What can I use it on?

iOS, Android, and the web. Everything stays in sync, so you can plan from your laptop and get reminders on your phone.

Who can see my plans?

You decide. Every plan can be private, friends-only, or open to anyone nearby. Nothing is public unless you make it public.

Is there a feed or an algorithm?

No followers and no algorithm. You can see what your friends are up to, but there's nothing to endlessly scroll, no strangers, and nothing picked for you by an algorithm. Local events come from the interests you choose, and that's the only recommendation we do.

What do you do with my data?

We use it to run the app and nothing else. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or build profiles on you.

How do reminders work?

Once a plan is set, everyone who's in gets a nudge as it gets close. Reminders exist to get people out the door, not to pull you back into the app.

What are groups and channels?

Every group of friends gets its own space, with channels for each plan, photos, and trip talk. Nothing important gets buried under 80 memes.

Still have a question?

We're a small team and we read everything. Ask us anything.
