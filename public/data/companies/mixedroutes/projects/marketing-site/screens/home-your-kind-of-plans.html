All FAQs

Is MixedRoutes free?

Yes. MixedRoutes is free to use on iOS, Android, and the web. You can keep using it for free.

Do my friends need the app?

No. They can view a shared plan in their browser and RSVP with a free account.

Who can see my plans?

You decide. Make a plan private, visible to your friends, or public, and choose whether to share it by link.
