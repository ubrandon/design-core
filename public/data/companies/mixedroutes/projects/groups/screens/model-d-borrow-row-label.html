Borrow from
a group
Pull members from someone else's group into your own plan, without posting to that group.
When
✓ I'm in Jake's "Valorant" group but only want to invite 4 of 8
✓ I want to plan with my "Hiking Crew" + my partner without posting
✓ Surprise birthday — invite group minus the birthday person
Two distinct verbs
Post to group — plan lives there, all members see it
Borrow members — plan stays yours, group never sees it
