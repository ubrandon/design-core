What's a circle?
A private label you put on your friends. Tag once, plan with them in one tap.
Only you see it
Friends never know they're in a circle. No notifications.
Friends can be in many
A coworker can be in "Coworkers" and "Gamers" at the same time.
Plan faster
Pick a circle when starting a plan to invite everyone in it at once.
Looking for a shared chat?
That's a Group. Members see each other and can chat together.
