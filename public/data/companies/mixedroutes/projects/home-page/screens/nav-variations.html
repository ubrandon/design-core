Bottom nav exploration

The full set of destinations doesn't fit five tabs. Two camps below: Camp 1 uses a "You" tab that absorbs Saved + Friends + Settings. Camp 2 gives Saved its own tab and moves the avatar/settings to the top.

Home — feed (default landing)
Discover — browse events
Plans — upcoming calendar
Chat — conversations
Saved — events + locations
Friends — list, add, share QR
Settings — accessible, not prime
Create plan — biggest target (FAB)
